01 — Foundations

One dark surface, one green voice

The whole app lives on a near-black ground with soft charcoal cards. A single vivid green carries every action: primary buttons, the active tab, focused inputs, and healthy money. Status colors are reserved for loan states and never reused for decoration.

03 — Screens

The eleven screens that run a group

High-fidelity mockups for every flow in the deck, populated with a realistic mid-cycle group: Umoja Women Group, cycle 2, 18 members, meeting weekly on Sundays.

Dashboard

At a glance: Total Savings, Active Loans, Members, Meetings, Fines and Social Fund — all live, with a savings trend chart for performance monitoring.

Group setup & configuration

A 4-step wizard — Basics, Savings, Loans, Schedule — captures the group constitution once: KSh 100 shares (max 10 per meeting), KSh 50 social fund, 5% reducing-balance interest, 2× savings loan ceiling, weekly Sunday meetings.

Meeting management

Every meeting is auto-numbered and digitally documented: 1-tap attendance, all money movements recorded in-session, then closed to lock the record into an immutable audit trail.

Savings & share management

Disciplined savings, transaction by transaction: fixed KSh 100 shares recorded instantly to the meeting ledger, with the social fund tracked separately.

Loan management

Smart lending, full visibility: eligibility auto-computed from savings before disbursement, due dates enforced, and every loan tagged Active, Repaid or Defaulted.

Member management

Register and manage group members, each with an individual savings and loan profile feeding the eligibility engine.

04 — Rules the UI enforces

The constitution, encoded

The design isn't just screens — it hard-codes the group's bylaws so the app, not memory or arithmetic, is the source of truth.

Loan eligibility

available = 2 × savings − active balance — computed and shown before the principal field accepts input. Over-lending is impossible.

Immutable meetings

Closing a meeting locks every record in it. Closed meetings are read-only forever — the audit trail can't be edited or falsified.

Fixed savings discipline

KSh 100 / share · max 10 / meeting plus a KSh 50 social fund contribution, tracked separately from savings.

Offline-first sync

Transactions queue locally and upload when connectivity returns. The amber cloud chip shows exactly how many records are pending.

Interest & term

5% reducing balance, default term 3 months. Due dates are fixed at disbursement to enforce repayment discipline.

Loan lifecycle

Every loan is always in exactly one state — active repaid defaulted — visible everywhere the loan appears.
